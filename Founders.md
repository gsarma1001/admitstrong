# Founder's Guide

## Why AdmitStrong Exists

No student should lose opportunities because they lacked access to guidance.

---

## Company Principles

1. Student First

Every decision starts with helping students.

---

2. Accessibility

Elite guidance should not require elite income.

---

3. Transparency

Recommendations should always explain WHY.

---

4. Personalization

No two students should receive identical roadmaps.

---

5. Continuous Growth

Every interaction improves the AI.

---

## Engineering Principles

Simple > Clever

Readable > Complex

Reliable > Fancy

Ship early

Improve continuously

Measure everything

---

## Product Philosophy

Don't overwhelm users.

Reduce anxiety.

Celebrate progress.

Always explain recommendations.

Be proactive rather than reactive.

---

## Decision Framework

Before building any feature ask:

Does it reduce stress?

Does it improve decision-making?

Can parents understand it?

Will students actually use it?

Would we recommend this to our own children?

If not,

Don't build it.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the world's most trusted AI education platform.
