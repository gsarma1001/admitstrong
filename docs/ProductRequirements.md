# Product Requirements

## Product Goal

Reduce uncertainty throughout the college admissions journey by providing proactive, personalized AI guidance.

---

## MVP Goals

Allow families to:

- Build a student profile
- Upload transcripts
- Receive personalized recommendations
- Discover colleges
- Track deadlines
- Receive AI reminders

---

## Core Features

### Student Profile

- Academic history
- GPA
- Courses
- Activities
- Awards
- Interests
- Career goals

---

### AI Recommendation Engine

Recommend:

- High school courses
- Competitions
- Summer programs
- Internships
- Volunteer opportunities
- SAT/ACT timeline

---

### College Planning

Recommend colleges using:

- Academic profile
- Cost
- Scholarships
- Fit
- Majors
- Location
- Acceptance probability

---

### College Comparison

Compare:

- Tuition
- Net cost
- Diversity
- Campus culture
- Academic strength
- Outcomes

---

### Notification Engine

Examples:

- SAT reminder
- FAFSA reminder
- Essay deadlines
- Recommendation letters
- Summer opportunities

---
